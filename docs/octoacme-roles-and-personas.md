# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons identify, engage, and manage all stakeholders involved in OctoAcme projects. They facilitate communication between internal teams and external stakeholders, ensuring requirements are understood and expectations are aligned throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder analysis and maintain stakeholder registry
- Facilitate stakeholder communication and manage expectations
- Gather, document, and communicate stakeholder requirements
- Provide regular updates and manage stakeholder feedback
- Identify and escalate stakeholder concerns and conflicts

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder engagement and satisfaction
- Reduce scope creep through clear requirements management
- Build strong relationships that support project success
- Maintain alignment between stakeholder needs and project delivery

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works closely with Project Manager**: Collaborates on communication plans and escalation processes
- **Supports Business Analyst**: Ensures stakeholder requirements are clearly captured and validated
- **Informs Product Manager**: Shares stakeholder feedback to influence product direction
- **Updates Developers**: Communicates stakeholder expectations and changes to the team

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and analysis sessions
- Regular stakeholder update meetings and newsletters
- Requirements documentation and feedback summaries
- Escalation reports to project leadership

---

## Compliance & Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance & Risk Officers ensure that OctoAcme projects adhere to regulatory requirements, organizational policies, and industry standards. They identify, assess, and mitigate risks while maintaining audit trails and governance compliance.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct compliance assessments and audits
- Identify, assess, and document project risks
- Develop and monitor risk mitigation strategies
- Maintain regulatory and compliance documentation
- Approve security and compliance-related decisions
- Escalate critical risks and compliance violations

### Goals
- Ensure projects meet all regulatory and compliance requirements
- Minimize organizational risk exposure
- Maintain audit readiness and documentation
- Support secure and compliant project delivery

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Project Manager**: Collaborates on risk management plans and mitigation strategies
- **Reviews with Developers**: Ensures code and processes meet security and compliance standards
- **Advises Product Manager**: Identifies compliance constraints that impact product decisions
- **Reports to Leadership**: Escalates critical risks and compliance issues

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessment reports and risk registers
- Compliance review meetings and audits
- Security approval processes and documentation
- Escalation reports for critical issues

---

## Knowledge Manager

### Role Summary
Knowledge Managers capture, organize, and share institutional knowledge from OctoAcme projects. They ensure best practices, lessons learned, and process improvements are documented and accessible to the organization for continuous learning and improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Document project processes, decisions, and outcomes
- Conduct and document lessons learned sessions
- Maintain and update the organizational knowledge base
- Develop training materials and best practice guides
- Enable knowledge sharing across teams and projects
- Identify knowledge gaps and gaps in documentation

### Goals
- Build organizational memory and institutional knowledge
- Reduce rework by enabling knowledge reuse
- Accelerate team onboarding and development
- Support continuous improvement through documented insights

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Collaborates with Project Manager**: Participates in retrospectives and captures project outcomes
- **Engages with Developers**: Documents technical decisions and best practices
- **Works with Product Manager**: Captures product learnings and market insights
- **Supports all teams**: Provides accessible documentation and training materials

### Typical Communication
- Lessons learned documentation and retrospective reports
- Knowledge base updates and maintenance
- Training sessions and onboarding materials
- Best practice guides and process documentation

---

## Process Improvement Facilitator

### Role Summary
Process Improvement Facilitators drive continuous improvement across OctoAcme projects. They analyze process efficiency, facilitate retrospectives, identify bottlenecks, and recommend optimizations to enhance project delivery and team effectiveness.

### Responsibilities
- Analyze project processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Facilitate project retrospectives and improvement discussions
- Document process improvement recommendations and outcomes
- Monitor implementation of improvement initiatives
- Track and measure process improvement metrics
- Share best practices and lessons across projects

### Goals
- Continuously enhance project delivery efficiency
- Reduce cycle time and rework through process optimization
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Enable teams to learn from each project iteration

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Facilitates with Project Manager**: Leads retrospectives and improvement planning sessions
- **Engages with Developers**: Gathers technical feedback on process barriers
- **Collaborates with Product Manager**: Identifies product delivery process improvements
- **Works across all teams**: Implements improvements and tracks outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Retrospective facilitation and reports
- Process improvement recommendations and documentation
- Metrics dashboards and progress tracking
- Cross-project improvement sharing sessions

---

## Customer Success Advocate

### Role Summary
Customer Success Advocates represent the customer voice throughout OctoAcme projects. They ensure that solutions meet customer needs, deliver value, and satisfy user expectations. They bridge the gap between customer requirements and internal project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Represent customer perspective in project decisions
- Validate that solutions meet customer needs and expectations
- Conduct user testing and gather customer feedback
- Communicate customer requirements and satisfaction metrics
- Identify and escalate customer concerns
- Ensure customer success metrics are tracked and achieved

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and value delivery
- Ensure solutions are fit-for-purpose and meet customer needs
- Build strong customer relationships and trust
- Reduce post-delivery support and issues through proper validation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Product Manager**: Ensures customer voice shapes product decisions
- **Collaborates with Project Manager**: Communicates customer expectations and satisfaction
- **Works with Developers**: Validates solutions and gathers feedback on usability
- **Supports Business Analyst**: Ensures requirements accurately reflect customer needs

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback sessions and user testing
- Customer satisfaction reports and metrics
- Requirement validation and sign-off documentation
- Customer success reviews and updates
